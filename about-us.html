About NetPulse Test

Empowering internet users worldwide with transparent, accurate, and multi-threaded network diagnostics.

Our Mission

Founded in 2026, NetPulse Test (https://netpulse.2bd.net) was created with a clear mission: to provide everyday internet users, remote workers, gamers, and IT professionals with a fast, lightweight, and completely unbiased connection diagnostic suite.

Modern internet activities—ranging from 4K HDR live streaming and cloud gaming to remote video conferencing on Zoom—require more than just raw bandwidth; they demand low latency and minimal jitter. NetPulse Test measures your complete digital pipeline in seconds.

Why Choose NetPulse Test?

  • Multi-Threaded Saturator: Unlike legacy single-socket tools, NetPulse opens 4 to 6 concurrent parallel streams to fully utilize gigabit fiber and high-speed 5G mobile bandwidth.
  • RFC 3550 Standard Jitter Tracking: We compute true packet delay variation so you can identify why your online calls drop or multiplayer games stutter.
  • Zero Forced Sign-Ups or Paywalls: NetPulse is 100% free with no registration, subscription fees, or invasive application installers.
  • Privacy-First Measurement: We do not log personal browsing activity, download history, or private data packets.

How We Sustain Our Service

To keep our high-capacity server nodes running globally and ensure our testing tools remain free for everyone, we partner with reputable ad platforms like Google AdSense. We are committed to maintaining clean, non-intrusive ad placements that never obstruct your testing experience.
