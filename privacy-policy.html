Privacy Policy for NetPulse Test

Website: https://netpulse.2bd.net | Last Updated: August 28, 2026

At NetPulse Test, accessible from https://netpulse.2bd.net, the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by NetPulse Test and how we utilize and safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect During Speed Tests

NetPulse Test is a browser-based diagnostic utility. When you run a diagnostic test on our website, we may temporarily process the following non-personally identifiable technical telemetry data:

  • IP Address: Used strictly to determine the nearest regional test server node, calculate accurate round-trip time (RTT/Ping), and resolve your Internet Service Provider (ISP).
  • Network Performance Metrics: Download bandwidth throughput, upload throughput, ping latency, packet jitter, and estimated bufferbloat.
  • Device and Browser Telemetry: Browser type, operating system, device screen resolution, and network adapter type (WiFi, Ethernet, Cellular 4G/5G).

Note: We do NOT collect or store sensitive personal information such as your name, home address, credit card numbers, or passwords.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the diagnostic telemetry data exclusively to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our speed test and packet latency services.
  • Accurately compute real-time connection bandwidth and quality scoring.
  • Prevent fraud, bot attacks, and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) abuse on our measurement servers.
  • Display historical speed test comparisons stored locally within your browser's LocalStorage.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, NetPulse Test uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences (such as selected speed units: Mbps vs MB/s, dark mode toggles) and pages accessed. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and device configuration.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Google AdSense

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to https://netpulse.2bd.net and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Some of our advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons on our site. Our primary advertising partner is Google AdSense. Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on NetPulse Test, which are sent directly to users' browser.

NetPulse Test has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

6. GDPR and CCPA Data Protection Rights

We ensure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights:

  • The right to access & erasure: You have the right to clear any stored local diagnostic history directly via the "Clear History" button on our app or by clearing your browser cache.
  • The right to opt out: California residents (CCPA) and EU residents (GDPR) have the right to request non-personalized advertising preferences.

7. Data Security

All communication with NetPulse Test is encrypted via industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption. Binary test payloads are streamed in real time without being permanently written to disk alongside identifying user credentials.

8. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at contact@netpulse.2bd.net.
